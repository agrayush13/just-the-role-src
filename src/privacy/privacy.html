JustTheRole privacy

Short version: JustTheRole changes how supported job pages look. It does not collect or transmit page content, account data, or browsing activity.

What is stored

Your Focus Mode switch and category choices are stored locally by Chrome on your device.

What is not collected

JustTheRole does not store job descriptions, profile information, resumes, companies, applications, messages, or account details. It has no analytics and makes no extension-initiated network requests.

Permissions

The extension requests local storage and access only to LinkedIn job URLs so it can apply your display preferences.

Independence

JustTheRole is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.
