JUSTTHEROLE privacy

Short version: JUSTTHEROLE changes how supported job pages look and marks criteria you explicitly provide. It does not collect or transmit page content, account data, or browsing activity.

What is stored

Your Focus Mode switch, preset, category choices, reading-tool preferences, on-page widget preference, and keyword rules are stored locally by Chrome on your device. Keyword rules may reveal career preferences and should be treated as sensitive configuration.

Optional Chrome Sync

Sync is off by default. If you enable it, your complete extension configuration, including keyword rules, may be stored and synchronized through your Chrome account. If Sync is unavailable, JUSTTHEROLE keeps the local copy.

What is not collected

JUSTTHEROLE does not store job descriptions, job URLs, titles, companies, profile information, resumes, applications, messages, search terms, browsing history, derived scores, or account details. It has no remote analytics and makes no extension-initiated network requests.

Permissions

The extension requests local storage and access only to LinkedIn job URLs so it can apply your display preferences.

Independence

JUSTTHEROLE is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.
